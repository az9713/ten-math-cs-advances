Appendix: notation glossary

SymbolMeaningDefined
$L(B)$, $\mathrm{dist}_2(t, L(B))$the lattice generated by the columns of $B$; Euclidean distance from target $t$ to its nearest lattice point§1
$\mathrm{GapCVP}^{(p)}_{\gamma}$the promise problem: decide $\mathrm{dist}_p(t, L(B)) \le r$ versus $\gt \gamma r$; hardness for $\gamma = n^{1/400}$ is the theorem§1, §3
$\varphi$, $m$, $\ell$, $\sigma$the 3SAT formula, its variable count, its clause count, a Boolean assignment $\sigma \in \{0,1\}^m$§2
$N$; $n$the common size parameter dominating $m$ and $\ell$; the lattice dimension of the produced instance, $n = M$§3, §14
$\mathbb{F}_q$, $q = 2^e$, $g$the characteristic-two field ($e \approx 200\log_2 N$, so $N^{200} \le q \lt 2N^{200}$); a fixed generator of $\mathbb{F}_q^\times$§5, §7
$a_1, \dots, a_m$; $P$the anchors, one distinct field point per variable; the remaining evaluation points $P = \mathbb{F}_q \setminus \{a_i\}$§6
$d$, $T$, $K$the degree budget ($d = m$ in the toy, $d \le N$ in general), the moment budget $T = N^{30}$, the fiber-size cap $K = N^4$§6–§7, §11
$Q_\sigma(X)$the unique polynomial of degree $\le m-1$ with $Q_\sigma(a_i) = \sigma_i$: the assignment, encoded as one curve§6
$\Theta$, $\tau$, $B_C$the table types: the global type $0$ and one type $(C, \beta)$ per clause $C$ and satisfying local assignment $\beta \in B_C$; $|\Theta| = 1 + \sum_C |B_C| \le 1 + 8\ell$§6
$x_{\tau,p,w}$the binary unknowns: table $\tau$, point $p \in P$, field value $w \in \mathbb{F}_q$; one-hot on the intended solution§6
$S_\tau(p)$the fiber: the set of $w$ with $x_{\tau,p,w} = 1$, i.e. the column of table $\tau$ above $p$§6, §11
$\mu_{\tau,j}$, $\eta_{\tau,i,j}$the ordinary and shifted moment polynomials: degree-$\le dj$ (resp. $\le (d-1)j$) interpolants of $\sum_{w \in S_\tau(p)} w^j$ and $\sum_w ((w-\beta_i)/(p-a_i))^j$§7
$\mathrm{RS}(P, D)$the Reed–Solomon code: evaluations on $P$ of polynomials of degree $\le D$§5
$Hx = b$, $M$, $R$the binary affine system collecting (C1)–(C4), bit-sliced over $\mathbb{F}_2$; its dimension $M = |\Theta||P|q$; the completeness weight $R = (\ell+1)|P|$§7
$C$, $k_0$the solution code $\{x : Hx = 0\}$ and its dimension§4
$W(H, b)$the minimum Hamming weight of a solution of $Hx = b$ (min over the coset)§4
$\Lambda_H$, $B_H$, $u$the parity-lift lattice $\{z \in \mathbb{Z}^M : Hz \equiv 0 \bmod 2\}$, its basis matrix, and the $\{0,1\}$-lift of a particular solution; Lemma 7: $\mathrm{dist}_2^2(u, \Lambda_H) = W(H,b)$§4
$h$, $G(Y)$, $\alpha_s$a fiber size, the degree-$h$ Hankel output polynomial, and its roots in an extension: the reconstructed “names” of the fiber elements§8–§9
$F = K(X)$, $E$the rational function field over $K = \mathbb{F}_q$ and the finite separable extension where the $\alpha_s$ live§9–§10
$\ell_a = X - a$, $\mathrm{ord}_{\ell_a}$, $v_a$the linear prime at $a$, its valuation on $F$, and an extension of that valuation to $E$ (values in $\frac{1}{e}\mathbb{Z}$, $e$ the ramification index — not the field exponent of $q = 2^e$)§10
$\mathcal{S}_\tau$; $R_0$, $R_{(C,\beta)}$the support profile $(S_\tau(p))_{p \in P_\tau}$ of table $\tau$ over its surviving points (after the Markov discard); the per-table root sets reconstructed from it§11, §13
$D_0$, $U_h$, $z$the degree and moment-window bounds of the extraction argument: $U_h = h^2 D_0 + dh^2 + 1 \le 4N^{17}$, window start $z$, $z + h - 1 \le 5N^{21} \lt T$§12
$1/200$, $1/400$the binary-code gap exponent forced by $M \approx q^2$, and its halving through the square root in Lemma 7§7, §14
