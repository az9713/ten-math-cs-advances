18. Numerical validation

Every load-bearing formula in this explainer has been recomputed independently — most of them in exact rational arithmetic, which turns validation into transcription-proofing: a single mistyped index in a formula like (74) or (111) makes an exact identity like “the weights sum to exactly $1$” fail loudly. Three of these deserve a word beyond the table. Check 12 is the serious one: formulas (74) and (111) are the only imported machinery deep enough that a transcription slip could silently corrupt the whole spherical half, and they are exactly the kind of many-indexed expressions that PDF extraction mangles; the exact-arithmetic triple (normalization, reciprocity, specialization) would detect any single-factor error. Check 15 replaces a five-parameter optimization by certificate checking — the printed tuples are hard evidence for the kissing bound $0.39661$, verifiable in four lines of arithmetic. Check 18 ties together §§13–15 end to end: residues, spectral function, dimension exponent, Stieltjes potential, tilted measure, and the constant $\lambda^*$ must all be mutually consistent for the identity to close.
