Two lower bounds for the permanent: superquadratic circuits, near-quartic formulas

A rigorous, self-contained walkthrough of Chapter 5 of Ten Advances in Mathematics and Theoretical Computer Science (PDF pages 114–153), written for a reader with a PhD in some quantitative field — but not necessarily algebraic complexity theory. Every provable step is proved; the handful of imported theorems are stated precisely and boxed.
